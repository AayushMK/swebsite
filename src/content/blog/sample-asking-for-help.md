Ask almost anyone how they are doing and you will hear the same answer. Fine. Okay. Busy. We have built an entire vocabulary for not answering the question.

Some of this is politeness. But some of it is something we learned very young: that struggling is private, that problems stay inside the house, that what will people say matters more than what we feel.

## Who benefits from the silence?

When nobody talks about a problem, everyone assumes they are the only one having it. That is the quiet trap. Silence doesn't make the struggle smaller. It just makes it lonelier.

Maybe the first step is not some grand campaign, but one honest answer the next time someone asks how we are.
